Privacy and policies

Updated 25 November 2026

Salah Waqt is made by SIOKRAFT. Your prayer times, reminders, and saved settings stay on your computer. You can use the app without an account.

What stays on your computer

The app stores the locations you save and your choices for prayer times, reminders, and appearance. It uses these settings on your computer, even when you’re offline. The app has no ads or built-in tracking. It doesn’t monitor your activity or send reports about how you use it.

To clear saved locations and preferences, open Settings → General → Reset App.

How the app finds your city

During setup, the app tries to find your location. If your computer shares its location with the app, Salah Waqt uses it locally. If your computer can’t provide a location, or you choose internet lookup, the app can ask an outside service to estimate your city.

The service sees your IP address, the number used by your internet connection. The app tries IPinfo first and can try IPWHOIS if that fails. You can pick a city from the app’s list without an internet connection.
